what it does

Everything a 404 doesn't tell you

Broken links don't announce themselves. They sit in old blog posts, resource pages, and navigation menus until a real user — or a search crawler — stumbles into them.

  • Full-site crawl
    Follows internal links up to your configured page limit. Normalizes URLs, skips fragments and trailing slashes, handles redirects. Finds what you'd miss in a manual check.
  • External link verification
    Every outbound link is checked too. Partner pages go dark. Blog posts link to content that moves. SnitchRoot catches all of it — not just your own 404s.
  • Scheduled weekly reports
    Set it once. Every Monday you get an email listing every broken link grouped by the page it was found on — so you know exactly where to go fix it.
  • On-demand scans
    Pushed a big update? Ran a migration? Trigger a scan immediately from the dashboard. Don't wait until Monday to find out what broke.
